# Hi, I'm Dennis 👋

Head of Engineering at **[Carhartt WIP](https://www.carhartt-wip.com)**, based in Berlin.

I've spent my career where product, technology and business meet — as a developer,
researcher, consultant and founder. These days that place is commerce: storefronts,
shopping apps and the many systems behind them.

> *„Ick mach wat mit Computern.“* — me, in sixth grade. So far, so good.

## What I'm working on

**At Carhartt WIP** I lead engineering for the brand's digital commerce:

- 🛒 the **B2C webshop** and the **shopping app**, on a composable stack built around
  commercetools, Next.js and Sanity
- 🔌 the **integrations in between** — product information, CRM, checkout, payments,
  stock and orders
- 🧱 the **foundations** — infrastructure as code, shared CI workflows, project
  templates and engineering metrics

I also act as a technical sounding board for neighbouring teams, B2B among them.

## What I care about

- **Teams that ship.** Small, frequent, unremarkable releases beat big launches. I pay
  more attention to how work flows — lead time, review time, what's stuck — than to
  how much of it there is.
- **Removing friction.** If a vendor has no sandbox, build a mock. If the same question
  keeps coming up, build a dashboard. Good tooling is the kind nobody has to think about.
- **Craft.** Software should feel at home where it runs, and code should explain why,
  not just what. Some of my side projects exist only to find out how far that goes.
